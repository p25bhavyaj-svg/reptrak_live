Methodology and limitations
  1. Collection (direct scraping, no paid scraping services): Google News RSS and Bing News RSS (6-month window), Reddit public search, Yahoo Finance daily prices (1 year), Google Play rating and newest reviews, Instagram public profile, X public syndication timeline. Each source is best-effort; blocked sources are shown in the status panel and excluded.
  2. Sentiment: VADER compound score (-1 to +1) with a business and finance lexicon extension.
  3. RepTrak dimensions: items are tagged by keyword rules; score = 3 + 2 × mean tone × n/(n+4), so small samples move towards neutral. Performance blends tone with share-price momentum; Products & Services blends tone with app-store rating. Overall score uses approximate RepTrak weights.
  4. Pulse: Esteem = overall score; Admire = performance, innovation, leadership; Trust = governance, workplace, citizenship minus a penalty for preventable-crisis coverage; Feel-good = community and review tone.
  5. Toolkit: eight stakeholders with scorecard weights, engagement channels, frames, early-warning triggers and response rules from the CCR live project. Negative items are classified into SCCT clusters (victim, accidental, preventable) with prescribed response postures.
  6. Limits: keyword tagging and lexicon sentiment are approximations; scores indicate direction and relative strength, not a licensed RepTrak survey result. Instagram and X often block anonymous requests from cloud servers.
